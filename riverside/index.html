Keeping Your Riverside HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

How old is your system? That number tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and also shows higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a miserable night.
